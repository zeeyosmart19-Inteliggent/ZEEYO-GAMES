School-safe by design

ZEEYO GAMES is a single static web page made for classrooms, libraries and study halls.

🚫

No ads or pop-ups

Nothing sells, tracks or interrupts you.

🔒

No accounts or personal data

Scores and settings stay in your own browser. Nothing is uploaded.

💬

No chat or user content

No strangers, no messages, no links out to other sites.

🎓

Learning built in

Typing, math and vocabulary games sit next to the arcade games.

⏱️

Break reminders

Optional timer nudges you to rest your eyes and get back to class work.

♿

Accessible

Keyboard friendly, reduced-motion mode, readable contrast.

Shortcuts: / search · Esc close game · P pause · F fullscreen. Please play only when your teacher says it is okay. Honest note: a few packaged games (VEXISHOT 3D, AriexCars 3D, Turbo Lanes, Smart Math Pro, VEXORUN) request fonts or the 3D engine from public CDNs. If a school network blocks those addresses, those games may look different or not start. All other games work fully offline.
