About

Smart Math Pro is a free math training game that runs from Kindergarten all the way to Calculus and beyond. It was designed and built by Zeeyo, an independent maker with interests in IT, coding, programming, cybersecurity and engineering. Level up through 9,999 levels, unlock achievements and become the MATH KING 👑.

Created by Zeeyo — Original Project

Smart Math Pro is Zeeyo’s original math-learning project, built around his vision of making mathematics engaging, accessible, and rewarding for learners. Zeeyo leads the project’s creative direction and makes the decisions that shape its design, educational experience, features, game modes, challenges, progression system, and overall identity.

From its Kindergarten-to-Calculus learning journey to its practice activities, achievements, and interactive experience, Smart Math Pro is developed as a project with its own structure and identity. Zeeyo is responsible for the project’s direction, decisions, and final published experience.
